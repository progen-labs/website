Open **Appearance** in the dashboard's top bar to choose how it looks.

```screenshot appearance-menu
The Appearance menu.
button: **Appearance** in the top bar opens the menu.
theme: **Theme**: Liquid Glass or Space.
mode: **Mode**: light, dark, or Auto to follow your system.
```

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Theme** | **Liquid Glass**: frosted, translucent panels, capsule buttons and a soft gradient backdrop. **Space**: a mission-control look with a starfield, indigo and cyan glow and tighter corners |
| **Mode** | **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**, which follows your operating system and changes live when it does |

```screenshot appearance-looks
The four looks of the same report.
glass-dark: **Liquid Glass, dark**, the default.
glass-light: **Liquid Glass, light.**
space-dark: **Space, dark.**
space-light: **Space, light.**
```

Both themes have full light and dark palettes, so there are four looks. Your choice is remembered in the browser and applied before the first paint, so the wrong theme never flashes.

Every look keeps text at readable contrast, and results are never conveyed by colour alone: severities and ratings always carry an icon and a word. Animations such as the rotating culprit sphere stop when your system asks for reduced motion.
